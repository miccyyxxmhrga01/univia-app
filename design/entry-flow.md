# Welcome and onboarding

Mode: Operate. Native Android. Scope: app launch, onboarding, sign-in, shared surfaces.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A campus organization app with an inviting, physical surface. A short logo introduction and useful onboarding replace the old role chooser.

OWN-WORLD: The supplied login image governs composition; the latest user-selected palette is violet and orange. Campus photography, white-edged lavender glass, soft violet shadows, violet-to-orange actions, orange accents and rounded Poppins type. Recessed pill fields and raised controls. Existing BISU and BBCO artwork stays intact.

STORY: Recognize BBCO and BISU, understand discovery, membership and community, then sign in. Team access is secondary on the sign-in screen. Completion skips onboarding on future launches.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Center the BBCO logo on a raised circular surface, then reveal the app name and organization tagline. BISU attribution sits below. Onboarding shares the login's campus photo, centered logo pair and glass material. One frosted panel contains the centered page artwork/copy and pinned progress/Next footer, with Skip above it. Pages swipe horizontally and scroll vertically when necessary. Login places centered logo discs and white campus lettering above its glass form. Registration and team access sit inside the student card. A white background fade starts at 80% of the scene height and reaches white at the bottom. Neither login nor onboarding displays a Back button; native system Back remains available.

FORM: The user-pinned reference (`branding/login-design-reference.png`) overrides generated style assignments. Native Canvas glass and vector waves; original logos and campus photo; locally bundled Poppins. Intro motion uses alpha and transforms only, a staggered 700ms ease-out entrance, 200ms ease-in-out settle and 300ms hold before a native window crossfade. Navigation follows completion, with no replay after interruption or recreation. Disabled system animation skips the introduction.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
